# Plans for the demo

The phased plans still open that the finished demo needs. Open work is listed in `docs/BACKLOG.md`. When a plan's last phase
ships, its "As built" note goes at its end and it moves to `docs/archive/`. Ideas kept for after the demo are in `docs/post-demo/`.

| Plan | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [`NOBILITY.md`](NOBILITY.md) | Common and noble troop trees, esquires, knighting a named man (N1-N5) |
| [`SIEGES.md`](SIEGES.md) | The siege overhaul: the real place as the field, capture points, layered defence, sallies, relief and terms (SE1-SE10) |
| [`KIT.md`](KIT.md) | Troops made of kit and skill: base man + Arms, Riding, Bowcraft + the gear they wear, for every troop (KT0-KT7) |
| [`TIERS.md`](TIERS.md) | Tiers that matter: what each tier can do, seen in battle and on the map, and why the campaign wants good troops (TI0-TI6) |
