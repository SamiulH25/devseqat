Tip

You fight in the front rank. Then you rise above the field and command it.

Ironfield is a medieval battle and campaign game where the general is also a soldier. You lead a free company through a war of seven realms, and you stand in the line with it.

The company is your legacy.

Each company keeps a roll of named men. They earn their names in battle: first over a wall, a horseman brought down, standing when half the company fell. Those you lose are kept for ever. Raise a company here and see how long you can keep them.

      Seven realms are at war. Choose who you ride for.

      Each has its own heraldry, its own temper and four lines of troops in its own kit. Pick a shield. This page will wear its colours.

      A man's worth is visible.

      Men are promoted one at a time. Each step up adds metal, then heraldry, then a cape and a plume, so a veteran company looks hard-won from across the field.

      The world runs without you.

      Lords recruit, patrol, besiege and hold grudges. Councils vote. Winter doubles the rations. Brigands crown kings, goblins dig in, and after a great battle the dead do not always stay where they fell.
